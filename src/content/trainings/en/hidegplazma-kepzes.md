## Don't just operate the device – understand what it does

Would you like to apply cold plasma technology confidently, safely and on a sound professional basis? Our training is designed for professionals who, beyond operating the device, also want to understand how the technology works, how to choose treatment parameters, the indications and contraindications, and how protocols are built.

The training is **not tied to a device purchase**: it can also be completed with your own or a third-party device.

## Training fee

- **Alongside a Skinlab device purchase: 99 995 Ft** – if you buy your cold plasma device from Skinlab, you can take the live-model training at a 50% discount.
- **With a third-party device: 199 990 Ft** – if you come with a device from another distributor or manufacturer, the training fee is the full price.

## 4-hour, individual 1:1 training

- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Time slot:** morning or afternoon
- **Group size:** 1 person
- **Practice:** on a live model
- **Model:** provided by us
- **Certificate:** FAR-system generated, with a B-number

## Not a conveyor-belt course – every minute is yours

No large groups, no waiting for the handpiece, and practice time isn't limited to a few minutes. Throughout the entire training, the instructor works with a single participant, so we can also discuss in detail topics related to your own device, your treatment situations and your professional questions.

## We provide the model – all you have to do is learn

You don't need to arrange a model yourself. We provide a suitable model for the duration of the training, so you can practice the techniques you learn under instructor supervision, in a real treatment environment.

## With your own or a third-party device

It is not a requirement that you bought your device from us. When registering, please send us the exact model of the device and, if possible, its technical documentation, so that we can check in advance which functions and protocols can be taught safely on that particular system.

## A certificate that counts

The training is held within the framework of the officially registered adult education activity of Skinlab Beauty Equipment Kft. (adult education registration number: **FKB/2024/001306**).

After successful completion, you receive a FAR-system generated training certificate with a B-number, certifying the device operation, theoretical and practical knowledge you have acquired.
